# Bill of materials v3: components sourced from DigiKey where possible

Status: adopted by `hardware.md`, `wiring.md` and the datasheet index (Task 20). Prices are estimates in USD.

**How this was sourced, and what it is worth.** DigiKey's own pages block automated fetches (HTTP 403), so every DigiKey part number, spec and price below comes from web-search listings of DigiKey pages, not from a page opened and read. Stock, price and lead time change daily and differ between DigiKey's regional sites. Open each link and confirm the part number, stock and the datasheet's limits before ordering. Items marked **VERIFY** are specific things to check first.

**Changes from v2.** The owner withdrew the Cree CMA1825 LED. That removes the 36 V boost-driver problem: the LED and driver below run directly from the 12 V rail, so the LED section of `wiring.md` keeps a 12 V in, constant-current driver layout. Reading the RECOM and Cree datasheets for Task 20 found two problems, described in the LED rows below and in `wiring.md` section 6: the RCD-24's PWM input is active low and limited to 1 kHz, and one of the two CXA1304 codes listed is an 18 V part that cannot run from this driver on 12 V.

## Cost

DigiKey-sourced build, about **$390 without the SSD** (about $460-480 with a 1 TB SSD for frames). Parts that moved the total up since v1 are the official TMC2209 modules ($38 vs about $16 for third-party modules), the 60 W adapter ($43 vs about $22) and the bigger heat sink. A budget swap (third-party TMC2209 modules, a generic 12 V 5 A adapter, a Pololu 5 V regulator) comes to about **$330 without the SSD**. The microscope lens, motors, HQ camera and mechanical parts are the same as before.

## Lead-time risks

Murata 5 V module 38 weeks (shown), official TMC2209 module 16-19 weeks, RECOM driver 10-17 weeks, 12 V adapters 12-28 weeks depending on the model. If time matters, take the budget swap and check stock on the day.

## What this means for the other docs

1. `wiring.md` section 6 now names the RCD-24-0.70 and records the datasheet findings (active-low PWM pin, 1 kHz maximum).
2. `hardware.md` and `datasheets/README.md` adopt this table.
3. **Open firmware issue (separate task):** the firmware's 20 kHz LED PWM is above the RCD-24's 200 Hz (linear) / 1000 Hz (limit) PWM range, and the pin is active low, so the duty would also need inverting. Options in `wiring.md` section 6; tracked in `docs/ROADMAP.md`.
